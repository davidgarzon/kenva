What I Do

I take the role, not a seat on the side

01

Embedded Product & Technology Leadership

I step into the leadership role your team is missing and work from inside it. Strategy, roadmap, hiring, architecture and delivery. I answer for what reaches production, the same way anyone on your org chart would.

02

AI That Actually Ships

For teams that want AI in the product and keep getting stuck at the demo. I lead the build with your engineers, from scoping and architecture to evaluation and rollout, until it runs in production. I have shipped agents, watched them fail, and rebuilt them with humans in the loop.

03

Zero to Production

New product, real constraints. I set up the team, the architecture and the compliance from the first week and take it live. At Prixz that meant a regulated health product in production in 30 days.

How I Work

I work inside your team, in your rituals, your repositories and your roadmap reviews, and I lead the people you already have, with or without a reporting line. When the work needs more hands, I bring in engineers, designers and data specialists I have worked with for years, and they join your team the same way. You get senior ownership without agency overhead.
