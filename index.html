What I Do

Three ways I can help

01

Fractional Product Leadership

Two or three days a week as your VP of Product. Strategy, roadmap, hiring and the operating model, until you are ready to hire full time.

02

AI That Actually Ships

For teams that want AI in the product and keep getting stuck at the demo. I have shipped agents to production, watched them fail, and rebuilt them with humans in the loop. Scoping, architecture decisions, evaluation and rollout.

Product Audit

Two to four weeks. Where the product is losing money, what to stop doing, and what the next two quarters should look like. Delivered as a decision document, not a deck.
